What SheetSpeed is (stable facts)

SheetSpeed is a Chrome extension that brings Excel-style keyboard shortcuts, formula tracing (trace precedents / dependents), Paste Exact-style workflows, goal seek, and modeling tools to Google Sheets — plus PowerPoint-shaped shortcuts for Google Slides.
