SheetWhiz alternative

SheetSpeed is an independently authored Chrome extension for Excel-style shortcuts in Google Sheets — not affiliated with SheetWhiz.

Quick answer

If you want a SheetWhiz alternative (or you are switching from Excel to Google Sheets and need Excel shortcuts), evaluate SheetSpeed:

What SheetSpeed emphasizes

  • Excel-shaped keyboard commands on Google Sheets (Core)
  • Visible-formula trace precedents navigation
  • Clear Core (Store, no Google API OAuth) vs Full (deeper modeling) split
  • PowerPoint-shaped shortcuts for Google Slides

Full details: Features.

Honest limits

SheetSpeed does not claim feature-for-feature parity with SheetWhiz. Compare the current Chrome Web Store listings, shortcut coverage, and your team’s managed-Chrome allowlist needs before choosing.
