SheetSpeed Data Processing Agreement

Product: SheetSpeed · Official website: https://sheetspeed.agi-jarvis.com/ · Chrome Web Store ID ljlmgielacploapelnjhodicgbjipcaa

Effective: 10 November 2026. Operator: Philip Dressler, doing business as Dressler Labs, California, USA.

This Data Processing Agreement (“DPA”) is between the organization or person that uses SheetSpeed as a business customer (“Customer”) and Dressler Labs (“Processor,” “we,” or “us”). It forms part of the SheetSpeed Terms of Service (the “Terms”). If there is a conflict about the processing of Customer Personal Data, this DPA controls. The Privacy Policy describes the product in more detail. Subprocessors are listed at /subprocessors/.

1. How this DPA is executed

This DPA is a standard, pre-executed offer. It is incorporated by reference into the Terms and applies when Customer accepts the Terms or otherwise uses SheetSpeed in a way that causes Dressler Labs to process Customer Personal Data on Customer’s behalf.

A countersigned copy is available on request. Email jarvis@agi-jarvis.com with the Customer’s legal name, a contact email, and any reference the Customer needs on the signature block. We will return a signed PDF.

This DPA is intended for enterprise and procurement review, including vendor forms. Once published, it replaces the previous statement on the enterprise review page that no pre-executed data processing agreement was offered.

2. Roles and an important limit on scope

Customer is the controller (or a processor acting for a third-party controller) of Customer Personal Data. Dressler Labs is a processor only where it actually processes Customer Personal Data on Customer’s behalf, as described in Annex A.

Spreadsheet, document and presentation content is not processed by Dressler Labs. SheetSpeed Core runs in the user’s browser, on the open Google Sheets, Docs or Slides page. Pro tools, after the user connects a Google account, call Google’s Sheets, Slides and Drive APIs directly from the browser under that user’s Google account. Dressler Labs operates no server that receives the user’s access token or the contents of the user’s spreadsheets, documents or slides. Those contents therefore never reach Dressler Labs, and this DPA does not treat Dressler Labs as a processor of that content. Google processes that content under the user’s (or Customer’s) own agreement with Google.

If the user turns on settings sync, shortcut settings are also stored in Chrome sync storage under the user’s Chrome profile. That is Google Chrome’s sync service for the user, not processing by Dressler Labs.

The local development AI helper is not included in the Chrome Web Store package, and the Store version does not send document contents to an AI provider.

3. Customer Personal Data we may process

When we act as processor, the processing is limited to:

  • Support and procurement communications that Customer or its users send to jarvis@agi-jarvis.com (message content, sender name and email address, and any attachments they choose to include).
  • Website technical data created when Customer’s users visit https://sheetspeed.agi-jarvis.com (for example IP address, user-agent, requested URL and referrer), which is handled by the website host and related vendors listed at /subprocessors/.
  • License and payment data, once paid billing opens (email address and plan status), processed by ExtensionPay and Stripe. During Pro Early Access we collect no license, account or payment data and do not contact ExtensionPay or Stripe.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. We do not claim SOC 2, ISO 27001, a designated data protection officer, insurance coverage, or any other certification or assurance that is not stated here.

4. Instructions and confidentiality

We will process Customer Personal Data only to provide, support and secure SheetSpeed, to comply with law, and according to Customer’s documented instructions in this DPA, the Terms and the Privacy Policy. Anyone we authorize to handle Customer Personal Data is under a confidentiality obligation.

5. Security measures

The following measures are factual descriptions of the current product and site. They are not a certification, audit report or guarantee:

  • The Chrome extension is Manifest V3. Extension pages use a content security policy of script-src 'self'; object-src 'none'.
  • Content scripts match Google Sheets, Docs and Slides document URLs, not all websites.
  • There is no SheetSpeed-operated backend for workbook or document content, and no AI provider in the Chrome Web Store package.
  • Pro tools obtain a Google access token through Chrome’s identity API after the user consents. The token stays in the browser and is used only to call the Google APIs listed in the Privacy Policy.
  • Connected Google API requests use HTTPS. There is no additional SheetSpeed encryption layer over Chrome local storage.
  • Shortcut settings and Pro undo or recovery records are stored in Chrome local storage on the user’s device. Undo records can contain cell values and formatting of affected ranges; the user can clear them in Settings or by uninstalling the extension.
  • The early-access Chrome Web Store package does not include the extensionpay.com content script and does not construct or call ExtensionPay.
  • The public website is a static site on GitHub Pages, served through Cloudflare, including a Cloudflare Worker for /sitemap.xml and /robots.txt. Live pages currently also load Cloudflare Web Analytics.
  • Vulnerability reports can be sent to jarvis@agi-jarvis.com as described on the Security page. We do not claim a bug bounty or a general support response-time agreement.

Customer authorizes us to use the subprocessors listed at https://sheetspeed.agi-jarvis.com/subprocessors/. We will post a change to that list before a new subprocessor starts processing Customer Personal Data, and will also notify Customer at the support address if Customer has asked to be notified of changes.

Customer may object to a new subprocessor on reasonable data-protection grounds within 30 days after notice. If we cannot reasonably accommodate the objection, Customer may stop using the affected part of the Service or terminate the affected subscription as provided in the Terms.

We will impose data-protection terms on subprocessors that are no less protective of Customer Personal Data than this DPA, to the extent applicable to the service they provide.

7. Assistance with data subject requests

Taking into account the limited personal data we hold, we will, at Customer’s request, reasonably assist Customer in responding to requests from data subjects to exercise rights under applicable law. Because spreadsheet and document content is not held by Dressler Labs, we cannot access, correct or delete that content. Customer should use Google’s own controls and the user’s device (including uninstalling the extension) for that data.

To ask about, access or delete personal data we do hold, email jarvis@agi-jarvis.com.

8. Personal-data breach notification

We will notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours, after becoming aware of a personal-data breach affecting Customer Personal Data we process. The notice will describe, to the extent then reasonably known, the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed.

9. Return and deletion

At the end of the processing, or on Customer’s written request, we will delete or return Customer Personal Data we hold, except where law requires retention (for example billing records, once billing opens, kept as long as tax law requires). Support emails may be retained as long as needed to complete the request and for a reasonable records period, then deleted on request where law allows.

We cannot delete data that was never sent to us, including spreadsheet content, local Chrome storage on a user’s device, or data held by Google under Customer’s or the user’s own Google account.

10. Audits

Customer may audit our compliance with this DPA by written questionnaire, no more than once in any 12-month period except after a personal-data breach affecting Customer Personal Data or where required by a supervisory authority. We will provide written responses and existing documentation that we can reasonably share. On-site audits and a right to commission a formal third-party audit are not offered as a standard term. We do not currently publish a SOC 2 report, ISO 27001 certificate or independent penetration-test report.

If a later official version of the Clauses or Addendum replaces those instruments, the replacement applies from the date it is required or the parties begin to rely on it.

To the extent the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the CPRA, or a similar US state privacy law applies, Dressler Labs is a “service provider” or “processor” to Customer. We will not sell or share Customer Personal Data, retain, use or disclose it for any purpose other than the business purposes specified in this DPA and the Terms, or combine it with personal information from other sources except as permitted for a service provider.   • Special categories of data. Not sought. A support mailbox may incidentally receive whatever a sender includes; we ask that sensitive data and confidential document contents not be sent.
