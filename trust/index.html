SheetSpeed trust pack

One URL for an internal decision. Send the section that matches the reader. You do not need to read the whole pack.

Start here (the champion)

About two minutes.

  1. Send your manager the decision summary and ask for a 15-minute OK on a free pilot, with a go/no-go date.
  2. Send IT this page before they ask. Say plainly there is no SOC 2 report; the controls are here.
  3. Fill the business case with your numbers (~20 minutes). Hard savings first. Hours saved are a soft estimate, not hard ROI. Include a downside case.
  4. Agree written success criteria and the mutual action plan. Start legal in parallel, then start the free, time-boxed pilot on a small group.

Ask how software of this size is approved at your company. There is no universal threshold, and a $0 pilot does not skip IT review.

What we do not have

  • No SOC 2 report. Also no ISO 27001, penetration-test report, named DPO, insurance claim in this pack, SSO, or SCIM.
  • No published change-of-control clause, source-code escrow, VPAT, or certification timeline.

Compensating controls — led by “spreadsheet content never reaches a SheetSpeed server,” and Core needing no Google API — are in the security overview.
