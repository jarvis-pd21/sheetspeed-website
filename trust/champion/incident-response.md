# Incident-response summary

**Matches the live DPA and Security page: 72-hour notice for a personal-data breach of data Dressler Labs actually holds.** Contact: jarvis@agi-jarvis.com.

## Scope

Dressler Labs does not host spreadsheet, Doc or Slide content. A “personal-data breach” here means unauthorized access to or loss of personal data **we actually hold** — for example, support email, billing identity once payment is on, or website analytics identifiers — not a leak of a workbook that never left Google and the user’s browser.

We cannot detect or notify you about a compromise of your Google account, your Chrome profile, or Google’s APIs. Those are Google’s and your processes.

## What we will do

1. Confirm whether data we hold was involved, and contain the issue (credentials, site, mailbox, or extension listing, as applicable).
2. **Notify the customer contact we have, without undue delay and at latest within 72 hours of becoming aware of a personal-data breach in scope.**
3. Share what we know: the nature of the incident, the data categories involved if known, steps taken, and recommended customer actions (for example, revoke Google access or uninstall).
4. For the Chrome extension itself, ship a Store update or, if needed, unpublish or warn via the site.

There is no 24/7 SOC and no dedicated incident team. The operator is the incident contact.

## What you can do without waiting

Revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, uninstall or Block the extension (ID `ljlmgielacploapelnjhodicgbjipcaa`), and rotate any Google credentials you believe are involved.
