# Decision summary — SheetSpeed pilot

**For a manager or budget owner. Fill the bracketed blanks, then forward. About 60–90 seconds.**
Light branding only. Prepared with the SheetSpeed trust pack. Not a vendor pitch deck.

**The problem and its cost.** We use Google Sheets, Docs and Slides, but our team still thinks in Excel shortcuts. We currently spend time on menus and workarounds [and, if applicable, $____ per year on ________________]. Leaving that as-is costs us [describe in one line: delayed models, extra clicks, a paid add-on, training time].

**The request.** Approve a **free, 30-day pilot** of SheetSpeed, a Chrome extension that adds Excel, Word and PowerPoint Alt shortcuts to Google Sheets, Docs and Slides, for [N] people in a pilot group, with a go/no-go on [date].

**What it is, in plain language.** People keep the keyboard habits they already have. Core shortcuts run in the browser and do not need a Google API connection. Optional Pro tools talk to Google from the browser only after a user consents. Spreadsheet content is not sent to a SheetSpeed server.

**Cost.** **$0 during the vendor’s early access, and $0 for this pilot.** Nobody is charged unless we later start a paid plan. After billing opens, published prices are $7.99/month, $69/year, or $149 lifetime. A filled business case with **our** numbers is attached or at the trust link below. Hours saved, if we show them, are a **soft estimate**, not a hard cost reduction.

**Key risk and mitigation.** The vendor does **not** have a SOC 2 report (or ISO 27001, or a penetration-test report). Mitigations: no SheetSpeed server holds our workbooks; Core needs no Google API access; permissions are limited to Google document pages and listed Google APIs; IT can pilot on a small OU and roll back by blocking the extension (Store ID `ljlmgielacploapelnjhodicgbjipcaa`). Refresh from Sheets, which briefly link-shares a temporary Drive image, can be left unused.
