# Time-boxed team pilot

**One page for the champion and the budget owner.** Cost during early access, and for this pilot, is **$0**. This is not a paid trial and not an open-ended rollout.

## What we are offering

- Duration: **30 days**, or shorter if you prefer. (If the review is unusually complex, agree **up to 4 weeks** in writing. Do not leave the end date blank.)
- The evaluation clock starts **after setup works**, not on the day someone says yes.
- Seat guideline for a first pilot: a small named group. **[DECISION: default cap 25 seats — operator has not locked this.]** Ask jarvis@agi-jarvis.com if you need a larger group.

- Security and legal start **in parallel**. Send https://sheetspeed.agi-jarvis.com/trust/ before day one.

## Before day one

1. Name the **economic buyer** and the champion.
2. Co-write **two or three measurable success criteria** (see the mutual action plan). Establish a baseline.
3. Confirm how software of this size is requested at your company. Do not assume a dollar amount skips IT review.
4. Send the IT pack. Decide whether Refresh from Sheets will be used (it briefly anyone-with-link shares a temporary Drive PNG).

## During the pilot

- **Weekly:** a 30-minute check-in and a one-page summary of what was tested, the result, and next week.
- **Midpoint:** are the criteria on track? If they are already met, you can go to decision early. Continue legal and security rather than parking them.
- **If use drops:** ask whether this is still a priority. Pause instead of dragging the calendar out.

## Written questions for week two and week four

Week two: Is install complete for the named group? Who has tried Core shortcuts, and who has (or has not) consented to Pro? Any security questions still open?

Week four / go/no-go: Did we meet the written criteria? Roll out, stay as optional, or uninstall? If billing later opens, do we have a commercial next step drafted?

## Go / no-go

Hold a scheduled meeting with the economic buyer against the written criteria. A possible go is not only “buy now”: early access is still free, and Core remains usable without a paid plan. A no-go means rollback: Block the extension or remove force-install, and users revoke Google access.

## After a go, when billing is open

Published prices are $7.99/month, $69/year, or $149 lifetime. Team invoicing, if offered, is a separate conversation — do not treat unpublished team rates as current. 14-day no-card trial is the published individual path once billing starts.

## Rollback (any time)

Admin: set SheetSpeed to **Block**, or remove **Force install**, in Devices > Chrome > Apps & extensions. User: `chrome://extensions` if not force-installed; revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Dressler Labs holds no copy of your spreadsheets.
