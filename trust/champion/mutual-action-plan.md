## Stall red flags

Treat these as a prompt to ask, not as an automatic cancellation:

- No economic buyer named, or “I will forward this to my team” with no evidence it was sent
- Security pack not opened; questionnaire requested but the public pack was not read first
- Weekly check-in skipped twice, or no one in the pilot group has installed it after setup was confirmed
- Legal or procurement left until the last week of the pilot
- Success criteria still unwritten after day one

If engagement drops, ask once whether this is still a priority. Pause rather than chase a dead pilot.
