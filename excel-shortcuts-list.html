SheetSpeed shortcut list

Every Excel-style shortcut SheetSpeed adds to Google Sheets. On a Mac, use Option for Alt and ⌘ for Ctrl. Alt sequences are pressed one key after another; Ctrl chords are held together.

FAQ

Does Google Sheets support Excel shortcuts without an extension?

Partly. In Google Sheets, open Help → Keyboard shortcuts and turn on "Enable compatible spreadsheet shortcuts". That adds many Excel-style Ctrl shortcuts, but not Excel's sequential Alt ribbon paths or trace precedents. Extensions such as SheetSpeed fill those gaps.

Can I change SheetSpeed's shortcuts?

Yes. Bindings are customizable in SheetSpeed Settings.
