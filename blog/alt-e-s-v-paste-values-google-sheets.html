Alt E S V in Google Sheets: Paste Values the Excel Way

Paste values is the most-used shortcut in finance. Here are both ways to do it in Google Sheets.

In Excel, Alt, E, S, V is a reflex: copy, then tap the four keys to paste values only. In Google Sheets, nothing happens. Here's what to do instead.

The native way: Ctrl+Shift+V

Google Sheets has its own paste-values shortcut: Ctrl+Shift+V (⌘+Shift+V on Mac). It pastes values only, without formulas or formatting. It's fast, free and built in.

For other paste special options (formulas only, formats only), use Edit → Paste special from the menu, or press Alt+/ and type "paste special".

The Excel way: Alt, E, S, V with SheetSpeed

If you'd rather keep the keys you already know, the free SheetSpeed Chrome extension routes Excel's paste special sequences to Sheets' native paste special:

Press and release each key in turn, just like Excel. On a Mac, use Option for Alt.

Why keep the Excel sequence?

If you work in both Excel and Sheets, one set of habits is easier than two. The Alt sequences also cover the formulas-only and formats-only variants, which Sheets has no direct shortcut for.
