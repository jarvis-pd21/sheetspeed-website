What is SheetSpeed?

SheetSpeed is a Chrome extension that adds Excel-style keyboard shortcuts, formula tracing (trace precedents), and modeling tools to Google Sheets — plus PowerPoint-shaped shortcuts for Google Slides. Official website: https://sheetspeed.agi-jarvis.com/. Chrome Web Store: Store listing. It is not sheetspeed.com, a Sheet Speed Checker, or a spreadsheet performance tool — see brand disambiguation.

Is SheetSpeed the same as sheetspeed.com or Sheet Speed Checker?

No. Unrelated entities share similar names. This SheetSpeed is only the Chrome extension for Excel shortcuts in Google Sheets (Store ID ljlmgielacploapelnjhodicgbjipcaa). Full clarification: SheetSpeed ≠ sheetspeed.com.

Yes. Is SheetSpeed a SheetWhiz alternative?

Yes — SheetSpeed is independently authored and can be considered a SheetWhiz alternative for Excel-like shortcuts in Google Sheets. It does not claim feature-for-feature parity; compare the current Chrome Web Store listings and SheetSpeed features.

Is SheetSpeed like Macabacus for Google Sheets?

Macabacus is primarily for Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint. SheetSpeed is a Sheets-native Chrome extension for people who want Macabacus-shaped / Excel-shaped productivity inside Google Sheets and Slides — not a Macabacus port or license.

Which browsers work?

Google Chrome is the supported target (114+). Chromium-based browsers may work; they are not the primary test surface.

Shortcuts do nothing — why?

Open the SheetSpeed launcher on the sheet and confirm shortcuts are enabled. Control chords are held together; Option/Alt sequences are pressed in order within a short window. Focus must be on the document, not an open dialog.
