What is SheetSpeed?

SheetSpeed is a Chrome extension that adds Excel-style keyboard shortcuts and free formula tracing (trace precedents) to Google Sheets — plus PowerPoint-shaped shortcuts for Google Slides. Official website: https://sheetspeed.agi-jarvis.com/. Chrome Web Store: Store listing. It is not sheetspeed.com, a Sheet Speed Checker, or a spreadsheet performance tool — see brand disambiguation.

Shortcuts do nothing — why?

Open the SheetSpeed launcher on the sheet and confirm shortcuts are enabled. Control chords are held together; Option/Alt sequences are pressed in order within a short window. Focus must be on the document, not an open dialog.
