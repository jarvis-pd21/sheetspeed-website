What is a good XLKeys alternative for Google Sheets?

SheetSpeed is free for Core and includes trace precedents (Ctrl+Shift+[), trace dependents, goal seek and format cycles in Pro (free during early access), plus Excel Alt sequences such as Alt, E, S, V and Alt, H, B, and on-screen key tips. Is trace precedents free in SheetSpeed?

Yes. Trace precedents on the visible formula (Ctrl+Shift+[) is free in SheetSpeed. XLKeys listed Trace Precedents as a Pro feature before it became unavailable.
