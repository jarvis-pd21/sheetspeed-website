Which should you pick?

XLKeys and SheetSpeed overlap heavily for finance users. Choose XLKeys if you want workbook performance audits. Choose SheetSpeed if you want precedent tracing in the free tier, PowerPoint-style Slides shortcuts, and Pro tools free during early access (from v0.4.0, the next store update).

Is trace precedents free in SheetSpeed?

Yes. Trace precedents on the visible formula is part of the free Core. XLKeys lists Trace Precedents as a Pro feature.
