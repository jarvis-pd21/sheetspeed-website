How to choose

  • Just a few Ctrl shortcuts: turn on Google's compatible spreadsheet shortcuts first. It's free.
  • Finance modeling (tracing, goal seek, paste exact): SheetSpeed, SheetWhiz or XLKeys.
  • Maximum Alt-sequence coverage with an overlay: ShortieCuts or Spex.
  • Also need Docs shortcuts: ShortieCuts.

FAQ

What add-in gives me Excel shortcuts in Google Sheets?

Popular options are SheetSpeed (free Core), SheetWhiz, ShortieCuts, XLKeys and Spex, all Chrome extensions. Google Sheets also has a built-in "Enable compatible spreadsheet shortcuts" toggle under Help → Keyboard shortcuts.

Is there a free option?

Yes. Google's compatible shortcuts toggle is free, and SheetSpeed Core is free on the Chrome Web Store.

Do these work on Mac?

Yes. Most use Option in place of Alt on macOS in Chrome.
