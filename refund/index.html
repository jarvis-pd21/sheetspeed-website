SheetSpeed Refund Policy

SheetSpeed is free on the Chrome Web Store and does not currently sell any paid plan, so there is nothing to refund today. The terms below apply only if you purchase a paid plan.

Yearly plans

If you purchase a yearly plan: full refund if you ask within 14 days of the first charge or of a renewal charge. Email us with the address you paid with, and we will refund through Stripe, normally within 5–10 business days.

Monthly plans

If you purchase a monthly plan: cancel at any time. You keep the paid features until the end of the current billing period, and you won't be charged again. We don't refund partial months.

One-time purchases

If you make a one-time purchase: full refund if you ask within 14 days of purchase.

How to cancel or request a refund

If you have a paid plan, open the SheetSpeed popup, go to Settings, then Manage subscription (the ExtensionPay/Stripe portal). Or email jarvis@agi-jarvis.com.

Your legal rights in your country, such as EU/UK consumer rights, still apply.
