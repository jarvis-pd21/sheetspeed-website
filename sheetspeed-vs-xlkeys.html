Which should you pick?

XLKeys and SheetSpeed overlap heavily for finance users. Choose XLKeys if you want workbook performance audits. Choose SheetSpeed if you want precedent tracing in the free tier, Paste Exact and PowerPoint-style Slides shortcuts.

Is trace precedents free in SheetSpeed?

Yes. Trace precedents on the visible formula is part of the free Core. XLKeys lists Trace Precedents as a Pro feature.
