FAQ

What is Pro Early Access? Right now every Pro tool is unlocked for everyone at no cost. Install SheetSpeed and use them; there’s nothing to buy and no card to enter.

What happens when billing opens? We’ll tell you first, in the extension and on this page, before any paid plan begins. Then you can choose monthly ($7.99), yearly ($69) or lifetime ($149). Once billing opens, new users get a 14-day free trial with no card. Nobody is charged automatically: you pay only if you start a subscription yourself. If you don’t, Core keeps working for free.

Can I cancel once billing opens? Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. Yearly plans and the lifetime deal come with a 14-day refund. See the refund policy.

Who will process payments? Stripe, through ExtensionPay, once billing opens. We never see your card. The early-access build does not contact ExtensionPay.

Do I need to sign in with Google? Core shortcuts do not. Pro tools ask you to connect Google, and nothing connects until you approve. During early access there is no payment account.

Which apps does it work in? Google Sheets, Google Docs and Google Slides, in Chrome. Docs shortcuts run locally on the page.

Can I change the shortcuts? Yes. Open SheetSpeed's settings for custom, remappable shortcuts, or press Ctrl+Shift+Space to search every command.
