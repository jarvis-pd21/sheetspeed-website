FAQ

Is SheetSpeed really free? Yes. The version on the Chrome Web Store costs nothing, and there is nothing to buy and no card to enter.

Do I need to sign in with Google? No. SheetSpeed asks for no Google account access. It uses Chrome's storage permission for your settings and activeTab for the document you're using, and runs commands through the page's own menus.

Which apps does it work in? Google Sheets and Google Slides, in Chrome.

Can I change the shortcuts? Yes. Open SheetSpeed's settings to remap shortcuts and cycles, or press Ctrl+Shift+Space to search every command.
