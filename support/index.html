SheetSpeed Support

Questions, bugs or feedback: email jarvis@agi-jarvis.com. We read every message and usually reply within two business days.

What to include

  • What you pressed or clicked, and what you expected to happen
  • Where it happened: Google Sheets or Slides, and Windows, Mac or ChromeOS
  • Your SheetSpeed version (the toolbar popup or chrome://extensions shows it)
  • A screenshot if you can. Please don't send spreadsheet or document contents you don't want to share

Common questions

  • Chrome turned SheetSpeed off after an update. An update that needs new permissions is paused until you approve it. Open chrome://extensions (or click the warning in Chrome's toolbar) and choose Accept permissions / Re-enable for SheetSpeed.
  • A shortcut does nothing. Click into the document first so the page has keyboard focus, then try again. On a Mac, use Option for Alt. You can check or change every shortcut in SheetSpeed's Settings.
  • Pro tools ask me to connect Google. Pro tools read and write the open spreadsheet through Google's API, so Google shows its own consent screen first. You can disconnect in SheetSpeed's Settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Pricing. Pro is free during early access. See pricing for what happens when billing opens.
