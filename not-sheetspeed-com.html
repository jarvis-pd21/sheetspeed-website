What SheetSpeed is

SheetSpeed (Chrome Web Store title: SheetSpeed – Shortcuts & New Capabilities for Sheets + Slides) is a Google Chrome extension that adds Excel-style keyboard shortcuts, formula tracing (trace precedents / dependents), and modeling tools to Google Sheets, plus PowerPoint-shaped shortcuts for Google Slides.

What SheetSpeed is not

  • Not sheetspeed.com or any unrelated “SheetSpeed” marketing site that is not hosted on sheetspeed.agi-jarvis.com.
