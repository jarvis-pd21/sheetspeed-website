Which should you pick?

Choose ShortieCuts if you want the widest coverage of Excel Alt sequences with a Key Tips overlay, plus Docs, and are happy to pay a subscription. Choose SheetSpeed if you want a free Core with no sign-in that includes trace precedents, plus Pro (launching soon) goal seek, what-if tables and trace dependents, with a planned lifetime option (Pro launching soon).

FAQ

Is SheetSpeed free?

SheetSpeed Core is free on the Chrome Web Store; Pro is launching soon at a planned $7.99/mo, $69/yr or $149 lifetime with a 14-day free trial. ShortieCuts offers a 14-day free trial and then costs $7.99/month or $69.99/year (per its site, Oct 2026).
